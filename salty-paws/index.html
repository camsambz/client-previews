Why a van, not a salon

A calmer groom for nervous, senior, and carsick pets.

At a traditional salon, pets can wait in a kennel for 4 to 6 hours and come home exhausted. With us, your pet is never more than a few steps from your front door.

One groomer, start to finish

Same groomer every visit, so your dog actually knows us. Most grooms are done in 60 to 90 minutes.

No cages, no hot-air dryers

Your pet has our full attention the whole time, with no other animals in the van.

A photo text when they're done

We send a photo when your pet is finished, so you know exactly when to come out.

About us

It started with a dog who shook the whole drive there.

Danielle groomed for 8 years in salons around San Diego and kept seeing the same thing: good dogs getting stressed out sitting in kennels all day. Her own rescue, Biscuit, would shake the whole drive there.

So she bought a used van, rebuilt it herself, and started grooming in driveways in Carlsbad. Word spread fast.

FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Do I need to be home?

Just at the start and the end. We need access to your pet and a parking spot near your door. We don't need your power or water.

How long does it take?

Most grooms take 60 to 90 minutes depending on size and coat.

What if my dog is matted?

We'll always choose comfort over length. Heavy matting may need a shave-down and a dematting fee, and we'll talk to you first.

Do you groom aggressive dogs?

We work with many nervous and reactive dogs. Let us know ahead of time so we can plan. We may stop a groom if it's unsafe for your pet.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours notice. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged 50%.

Do you groom cats?

Yes, on Tuesdays and Thursdays only, so the van is dog-free and quiet.

Are vaccinations required?

Rabies vaccination is required for all pets.
